Ask us directly ↗
Which sweets are available?+

We currently make Bengali-style Rasgulla and Gulab Jamun. Both are ₹15 per piece.

How do I place an order?+

Add sweets to your basket, complete the delivery form, then send the prepared message on WhatsApp. Your order is confirmed when we reply.

Why do you need 6 hours' notice?+

Our sweets are made in small batches. At least six hours helps us prepare your order with care. We check your requested time before confirming.

Which areas do you deliver to?+

We deliver to selected Mumbai locations. Share your area and PIN code and we'll let you know if delivery is available.

How much is Porter delivery?+

Porter charges depend on the delivery details and will be confirmed separately before your order is finalised.

How should I store my sweets?+

Storage guidance can vary. Please message us on WhatsApp and we'll share the right guidance for your order.

When is my order confirmed?+

Submitting the form opens WhatsApp with your order details. We confirm availability, delivery and charges with you there. Sending the message does not itself confirm the order.
